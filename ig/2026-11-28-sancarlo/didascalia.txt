Red and gold since 1737 🎭

The @teatrosancarlo opened on 4 November 1737, the name day of Charles of Bourbon: 41 years before La Scala and 55 before La Fenice. It is the oldest opera house in the world still in use.

In 1816 a fire gutted the hall during a rehearsal. Antonio Niccolini rebuilt it in about ten months and it reopened on 12 January 1817. Look up from the stalls and every tier of boxes turns towards the crowned royal box, where the kings sat facing the stage.

Guided tours run every day in Italian and English, around rehearsals, and the new opera season opens at the end of November. Before or after, stop for a coffee at @gambrinus_napoli, walk through @palazzorealenapoli_ufficiale next door, or grab a warm sfogliatella at @lasfogliatellamary in Galleria Umberto I.
