Sunday in Naples · Salsicce e friarielli 🌿

November opens friarielli season in Naples: the young, bitter flower shoots of Brassica rapa, grown in Campania and sold from late autumn to early spring. With pork sausage they make one of the great classics of Neapolitan tradition.

The name comes from "frijere", Neapolitan for to fry: friarielli go into the pan raw, with olive oil, garlic and chilli. Before they were called mangiamaccheroni, pasta eaters, Neapolitans were known as mangiafoglie, leaf eaters.

A versatile pairing: the sandwich, or its variant the cuzzetiello, and pizza are the two most common ways to eat them. The sandwich is filled with sausage and friarielli; the pizza is topped with fior di latte, sausage and friarielli.

🇮🇹 In breve · salsicce e friarielli
Novembre apre la stagione dei friarielli, i germogli amari della cima di rapa coltivati in Campania. Il nome viene da "frijere": vanno in padella a crudo con olio, aglio e peperoncino. Il panino, o la sua variante, il cuzzetiello, e la pizza sono i due modi più comuni di mangiarli. Dove trovarli domenica: 'O Cuzzetiello @ocuzzetielloloriginaledidario in via Rimini, Il Gobbetto @osteriailgobbetto (solo a pranzo), Al 53 @ristoranteal53 in piazza Dante e Lombardi 1892 @lombardi1892 in via Foria.
