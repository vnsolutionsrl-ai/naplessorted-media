Weekend in Naples · Eat & drink 🥐

Friday, Via Toledo: coffee at Ceraldi Caffè @ceraldicaffe in Piazza Carità 14, then a sfogliatella at La Sfogliatella Mary @lasfogliatellamary at the Via Toledo entrance of the Galleria Umberto I.
Friday evening: a spritz at Cammarota Spritz @cammarotaspritz, Vico Lungo Teatro Nuovo 31, in the Quartieri Spagnoli. Just before the Morricone concert.
Saturday, coming down from Capodimonte into the Sanità: a fiocco di neve at Pasticceria Poppella @pasticceriapoppella, Via Arena della Sanità 24. A soft brioche filled with ricotta and cream, first made right here.
Then Pizzeria Starita @pizzeriastarita, Via Materdei 27/28. Here since 1901. Order the montanara, dough fried and then finished in the wood oven.
Sunday morning, before the train to Herculaneum: Scaturchio @scaturchio_napoli in Piazza San Domenico Maggiore 19, baking since 1905. Try the ministeriale, a dark chocolate medallion with a liqueur cream inside.
Sunday lunch, Herculaneum: Pappamonte @pappamonte, Via IV Novembre 2/4, right opposite the entrance to the excavations. Try the pasta with potatoes and provola.
