Weekend in Naples · 20–22 November 🏛️

Friday, Via Toledo: last day of ‘Doppia scena’ at Sant’Anna dei Lombardi @santannadeilombardi, where Nicholas Tolosa shows his work inside the Cappella Tolosa. Admission is free. A few steps along, ‘Angelica and Élisabeth. The Age of Women Painters’ at the Gallerie d’Italia @gallerieditalia, Via Toledo 177: more than 70 works by Angelica Kauffmann and Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun, with their self-portraits from the Uffizi. Until 7 March: Tuesday to Friday 10–19, weekends 10–20, tickets €8.
Friday evening: the music of Ennio Morricone by candlelight in the Anglican Church @candlelight.concerts, tickets from €22.
Saturday: Capodimonte @museoboscocapodimonte, with Caravaggio’s Flagellation, the new Porcelain Gallery and ‘Atlas Partenope’ by Andrea Fogli. Open 8:30–19:30, last entry 18:30. Then a walk in the Real Bosco, 134 hectares of royal park.
Sunday: Herculaneum @ercolanoscavi. After decades behind closed doors, the House of the Mosaic Atrium, whose floor still ripples from the eruption of AD 79, and the Timber-Frame House, a shop with a home above, are open again. 8:30–17:00, last entry 15:30.
Where to eat along the way: our Eat & drink post, tonight at 6pm.
