Weekend in Naples · 20–22 November 🏛️

Friday: last day of the Nicholas Tolosa exhibition at Sant'Anna dei Lombardi @santannadeilombardi, with his work inside the Cappella Tolosa. Admission is free. In the evening, the music of Ennio Morricone by candlelight in the Anglican Church of Naples @candlelight.concerts, tickets from €22.
Saturday: Capodimonte @museoboscocapodimonte, with Caravaggio's Flagellation, 'Atlas Partenope' and the new Porcelain Gallery. Open 8:30–19:30, last entry 18:30. Then a walk in the Real Bosco, the royal park around the palace: 134 hectares, with views over the city and the bay.
Sunday: Herculaneum @ercolanoscavi. After decades behind closed doors, the House of the Mosaic Atrium, whose floor still ripples from the eruption of AD 79, and the two-storey Timber-Frame House, a shop with a home above, are open again. 8:30–17:00, last entry 15:30.
Where to eat along the way: our Eat & drink post, tonight at 6pm.
